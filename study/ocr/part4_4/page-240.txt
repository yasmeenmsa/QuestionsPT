PTEXAM THREE: QUESTION 85
Strengthening of which muscle group would be MOST beneficial for
this patient?

1. Strengthening of the rectus abdominis

2. Strengthening of the internal and external obliques

3. Strengthening of the quadratus lumborum

4. Strengthening of the erector spinae

Correct Answer: 4 (Goodman Differential Diagnosis

p. 481)

Since ankylosing spondylitis is associated with an increase in
thoracic kyphosis and loss of the lumbar curve, strengthening of
the trunk extensors is most beneficial. The patient has strength
within functional limits. However, additional muscle strengthening
would likely remain beneficial since it would promote a more
upright posture.

1. The rectus abdominis will produce a flexion moment at the
trunk. This will further accentuate the thoracic kyphosis and
decrease the lumbar lordosis associated with ankylosing
spondylitis.

2. The internal and external obliques when contracting bilaterally
will produce a flexion moment of the trunk, and when
contracting unilaterally will produce a rotary moment of the
trunk.

3. The quadratus lumborum will produce a lateral bending
of the trunk when performed in a closed-chain activity
and a hip hiking movement when performed in an open-
chain activity. These motions would not be the emphasis
of a strengthening program for a patient with ankylosing
spondylitis. The quadratus lumborum also assists with spinal
extension. However, the muscle is just one of many muscles
that serve this function.

4. The erector spinae muscles (i.e., iliocostalis, longissimus,
spinalis) acting collectively, serve as prime movers for
spinal extension. Extension exercises are often an important
component of a comprehensive plan of care to assist
patients with ankylosing spondylitis to maintain the normal
curves of the spine while at the same time limiting the
forward bending nature of the disease process.

PTEXAM THREE: QUESTION 86
What is the MOST appropriate justification to include a general
exercise program in this patient’s plan of care?

1. Exercise typically will reduce symptoms and reduce the risk of
future pulmonary complications

2. Exercise typically does not affect symptoms, but is necessary
to prevent postural deformities

3. Exercise typically exacerbates symptoms, but is necessary to
reduce the risk of pulmonary complications

4. Exercise typically will normalize the patient’s weight
Correct Answer: 1 (Goodman Pathology p. 1247)
Many patients with painful conditions are apprehensive about
beginning an exercise program. To increase the likelihood of
patient compliance, the therapist should take care in explaining
recommended interventions, anticipated responses, and intended
outcomes to every patient. Exercise is not only an important factor
in maintaining posture, but also greatly influences the patient’s
long-term prognosis by supporting aerobic capacity, muscle
function, and flexibility.

1. Exercise typically reduces complaints of pain and stiffness
in patients with ankylosing spondylitis. Exercise is especially
beneficial before significant ossification begins to occur as
it can greatly support maintenance of normal posture, which
in turn maintains space within the thoracic cavity allowing
for optimal lung expansion. Additionally, individuals with
ankylosing spondylitis are at increased risk of pulmonary
fibrosis. Reduced mobility in the thoracic region and the
possibility of pulmonary fibrosis development can be
mitigated with regular aerobic exercise.

2. Exercise typically will reduce symptoms throughout the disease
process and diminish the extent of postural deformities.

3. Appropriately selected exercises will not typically exacerbate
symptoms of ankylosing spondylitis. For patients with signifi-
cant symptoms, warm-up activities should be integrated to
ensure optimal exercise tolerance and benefit.

4. Patients with ankylosing spondylitis may gain weight because
of inactivity. However, exercise alone will not normalize
a patient’s weight. A balanced diet avoiding sugars, salt,
processed foods, and saturated fats, in addition to exercise,
is the most appropriate plan to decrease weight.
