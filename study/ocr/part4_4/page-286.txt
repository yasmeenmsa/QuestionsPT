PTEXAM THREE: QUESTION 175
Using the Rancho Los Amigos Levels of Cognitive Functioning Scale,
what would be the MOST appropriate classification of the patient?

1. Level!

2. Level IV

3. Level VI

4. Level VIII

Correct Answer: 3 (O’Sullivan p. 827)

The Rancho Los Amigos Level of Cognitive Functioning Scale is

descriptive scale used to assess cognitive and behavioral recovery

in individuals with a traumatic brain injury as they emerge from

a coma. There are eight traditional categories used to examine

progress after a brain injury. Patients may plateau at any level.

1. Level is “No Response.” At this level, the patient appears
to be in a deep sleep and is completely unresponsive to any
stimuli.

2. Level IV is “Confused-Agitated.” At this level, the patient is
in a heightened state of activity. Behavior is bizarre and non-
purposeful. Verbalizations are frequently incoherent and/
or inappropriate. Gross attention is very brief and selective
attention is often nonexistent.

3. Level VI is “Confused-Appropriate.” At this level, the patient
shows goal-directed behavior, but is dependent on external
input. The patient can follow simple directions consistently
and shows carryover for relearned tasks. Responses are
appropriate to the situation, but may be incorrect due to
memory problems.

4. Level VIIl is “Purposeful-Appropriate.” At this level, the
patient is able to recall and integrate past and recent events.
The patient shows carryover for new learning and needs no
supervision once activities are learned. The patient may still
show decreased abstract reasoning, judgment in unusual
circumstances, and tolerance to stress.
