PTEXAM ONE: QUESTION 122
During a balance assessment of a patient with left hemiplegia, it
is noted that in sitting the patient requires minimal assistance to
maintain the position and cannot accept any additional challenge.
What grade should the physical therapist use to document this
patient’s sitting balance?

1. Normal

2. Good

3. Fair

4. Poor

Correct Answer: 4 (O’Sullivan p. 212)

Sitting balance can be graded in an objective manner by using

a scale that ranges from poor to normal. A patient that requires

assistance to maintain a sitting position would be graded as

having poor sitting balance.

1. A grade of normal is indicative of a person that is able to sit
unsupported, move in and out of the base of support, and
accept maximal challenge without loss of balance.

2. A grade of good is indicative of a person that is able to sit
unsupported, move in and out of the base of support, and
accept some challenge without loss of balance.

3. Agrade of fair is indicative of a person that is able to maintain
their balance in sitting unsupported, but cannot accept any
challenge or go outside of their base of support without loss
of balance.

4. A grade of poor is indicative of a person that is unable to
maintain their balance in sitting without external support
or assistance.

PTEXAM ONE: QUESTION 123

A physical therapist performs

postural drainage ona
specific lung segment using

the technique depicted in the

video. Ideally, how should the

bed be positioned based on the
specific lung segment being
treated?

1. Elevate the head of the bed 6 inches
2. Elevate the head of the bed 12 inches
3. Elevate the foot of the bed 6 inches
4. Elevate the foot of the bed 12 inches
Correct Answer: 4 (Roy p. 539)
Postural drainage is the assumption of one or more body positions
that allow gravity to drain secretions from each of the patient’s
lung segments. In each position, the segmental bronchus of the
area to be drained is positioned perpendicular to the floor. The
goal of treatment is to mobilize the secretions from the smaller,
peripheral segments to the larger, more central airways. Based on
the location where percussion is being administered in the video,
the lung segment being treated is the right middle lobe. Postural
drainage promotes secretion mobilization and is indicated for
patients who are unable to mobilize their own secretions due to
weakness or illness.

1. Elevating the head of the bed is not associated with
performing postural drainage to the right middle lobe.

2. Elevating the head of the bed is not associated with
performing postural drainage to the right middle lobe.

3. Typically, the foot of the bed is elevated 12 or 18 inches (not
6 inches). Elevating the foot of the bed is desirable when
targeting the right middle lobe, lingula of the left upper lobe,
anterior basal segments of the lower lobes, posterior basal
segments of the lower lobes, and lateral basal segments of
the lower lobes.

4. Treating the right middle lobe requires the patient to
be turned from supine on the left side with the foot of
the bed elevated 12 inches. Percussion and vibration are
performed over the right chest between the axilla and the
right nipple. The lingula of the left upper lobe requires
percussion in a similar area of the body (i.e., between the
axilla and the nipple). However, the lingula would be on the
patient’s left side.
