PTEXAM TWO: QUESTION 85
Which of the following conditions is MOST likely to occur during the
late stage of the patient’s disease?

1. Meningitis

2. Pneumonia

3. Deep vein thrombosis

4. Septic shock

Correct Answer: 2 (Goodman Pathology p. 1412)

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis is an adult-onset progressive

neurodegenerative disease. It is characterized by an onset of

ascending muscle weakness beginning in the distal musculature
and progressing to respiratory muscles. Death usually occurs
within five years from the onset of the disease.

1. Meningitis is an acute inflammation of the membranes that
cover the brain and spinal cord. The condition is caused by
a bacterial or viral infection that reaches the meninges. This
condition is not associated with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.

2. Pneumonia is an inflammatory condition of the lung that
primarily affects the alveoli. Patients with amyotrophic
lateral sclerosis can have weakness of respiratory muscles
with decreased lung function leading to infection (i.e.,
pneumonia). Pneumonia in the final stages of amyotrophic
lateral sclerosis is associated with death.

3. Deep vein thrombosis (DVT) is the formation of a blood clot
in the deep veins of the legs. One of the most common risk
factors for DVTs is inactivity or immobilization, especially
after a major surgery. Though a patient with amyotrophic
lateral sclerosis may be immobile secondary to significant
muscle weakness, DVT is less common than pneumonia.

4. Septic shock is a condition that results from severe infection
and sepsis and leads to multiple organ failure. In severe
cases of pneumonia, septic shock may result, though it is
less common than pneumonia for patients with amyotrophic
lateral sclerosis.

PTEXAM TWO: QUESTION 86
During the current therapy session, the physical therapist becomes
concerned that the patient might be contemplating suicide based on
several verbal statements. Which of the following actions would be
the MOST appropriate initial step by the therapist?

1. Remind the patient it normally takes a period of time for this
type of medication to become effective

2. Discuss the impact suicide would have on the patient’s family
as a deterrent

3. Recommend the patient make an appointment to return to the
physician

4. Ask questions to determine if the patient is considering
suicide

Correct Answer: 4 (Goodman Pathology p. 87)

Health care professionals treat people, not diagnoses, and therefore

greater attention is placed on concepts of whole systems healing and

integrative care along with prevention. Side effects of antidepressant
agents are common and may affect multiple systems. All suicidal
thoughts must be taken seriously and responded to appropriately.

1. Although it is true that most drugs used to treat depression
take a period of time before reaching a therapeutic dose
and elevating mood, reminding the patient of this does not
address the therapist’s immediate concern for the patient’s
safety.

2. The temptation to offer reasons for living to the patient or
discussing the impact this might have on the family should be
avoided. The patient would benefit most from the therapist
conveying without judgement that they care and understand
that depression can be treated.

3. Although referral to the physician may certainly be warranted,
the therapist is clearly concerned that the patient may be
contemplating suicide. As a result, the therapist cannot
simply rely on the patient making an appointment with the
physician. If the option involved the therapist taking a more
active role in arranging for a physician visit, this option would
be more attractive.

4. Asking questions to determine if the patient is considering
suicide is recommended to determine the seriousness of the
concern for suicide. Health professionals should not hesitate
to ask whether a person is considering suicide or even if the
patient has a plan. The QPR model for suicide prevention
involves “Q” (question the person about suicide), “P”
(persuade the person to get help), and “R” (refer for help by
contacting the individual’s physician or making a referral).
