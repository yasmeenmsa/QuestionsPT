PTEXAM ONE: QUESTION 133
Which examination finding would be considered the MOST
confirmatory for the given diagnosis?

1. Back symptoms increase with spinal extension overpressure

2. Back and leg symptoms increase with spinal extension
overpressure

3. Back symptoms increase with spinal flexion overpressure

4. Back and leg symptoms increase with spinal flexion
overpressure

Correct Answer: 2 (Dutton p. 1361)

Lumbar spinal stenosis refers to a narrowing of the spinal canal

in the lumbar spine. Symptoms typically result from mechanical

compression on either the spinal cord or exiting nerve roots and
may be further exacerbated by bony degeneration or instability.

Structural changes may include degeneration of the vertebral

segments, disk herniation, osteophyte formation, and hypertrophy

of structures such as the ligamentum flavum.

1. Patients with spinal stenosis may experience back symptoms
with extension overpressure due to the combination of
forced compression and the positional narrowing of the
spinal column. However, the combination of back and leg
symptoms would provide stronger confirmatory evidence of
spinal stenosis.

2. Patients with spinal stenosis often experience back and
leg symptoms with extension overpressure due to the
combination of forced compression and the positional
narrowing of the spinal column. This pattern of symptoms is
classically associated with spinal stenosis.

3. Patients with spinal stenosis may experience back symptoms
with extension overpressure, but are far less likely to
experience symptoms with flexion overpressure due to the
positional widening of the foramen.

4. Patients with spinal stenosis may experience back and leg
symptoms with extension overpressure, but are far less likely
to experience symptoms with flexion overpressure due to the
positional widening of the foramen.
