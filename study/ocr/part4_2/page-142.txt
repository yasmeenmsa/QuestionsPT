PTEXAM TWO: QUESTION 84
Which intervention would be the MOST appropriate to include in the
patient's plan of care?

1. Passive range of motion

2. Active range of motion

3. Resistance exercise program

4. Endurance training

Significant loss of anterior horn cells produces progressive

weakness and muscle atrophy. The clinical presentation

is most consistent with the “middle” stage of amyotrophic lateral
sclerosis (e.g. widespread muscle weakness, loss of muscle mass,
contractures).

1. Passive range of motion is more appropriate as a primary
activity for the “late” stage of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis
when active exercise is no longer feasible. A therapist may
incorporate stretching activities at the current stage, but this
‘would not be a primary emphasis.

2. Active range of motion is often indicated to assist with
activities of daily living as long as the patient does not
exercise to the point of fatigue. Active exercise at low-
intensity evels has been found to be beneficial for patients
through the and “middle” stages of amyotrophic
lateral sclerosis.

3. Resistance exercise is not indicated for patients with
amyotrophic lateral sclerosis that demonstrate existing
weakness in muscles of 3/5 strength or below since it is
likely to result in overuse fatigue. A weak muscle is more
susceptible to overuse damage during resistance exercise
since the muscle s already functioning near its maximal limit.
