PTEXAM TWO: QUESTION 87
What muscles are MOST commonly associated with this medical
diagnosis?

1. Abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis longus

2. Abductor pollicis brevis and extensor pollicis brevis

3. Abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis

4. Abductor pollicis brevis and extensor pollicis longus

Correct Answer: 3 (Dutton p. 786)

De Quervain’s tenosynovitis results from an inflammatory process

involving the tendons and synovium of the abductor pollicis

longus and extensor pollicis brevis muscles at the base of the
thumb. The onset is typically due to repetitive activities involving
thumb abduction and extension.

1. The abductor pollicis longus is in the first dorsal compartment
and is associated with de Quervain’s tenosynovitis. The
extensor pollicis longus is in the third dorsal compartment
and is not associated with this medical condition.

2. The abductor pollicis brevis is not associated with de
Quervain’s tenosynovitis. However, the extensor pollicis
brevis is associated with the medical condition.

3. The abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis
are in the first dorsal compartment and are the muscles
involved with de Quervain’s tenosynovitis.

4. The abductor pollicis brevis and extensor pollicis longus are
not associated with de Quervain’s tenosynovitis.
